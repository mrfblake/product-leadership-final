## 1. Business case

_Why this initiative is worth funding over the alternatives. Include the key unit economics assumptions, CAC, LTV, payback period, where relevant._

| Assumption | Value | Source / rationale |
|---|---|---|
| CAC | $25,000 | Blended estimate of marketing, sales, and grassroots Customer Success efforts required to land a new mid-market account or drive field adoption at an existing Top 100 GC. |
| LTV | $250,000 | Assumes an average $50,000 ACV (balancing large enterprise expansion with mid-market entry) over a conservative 5-year retention lifespan once the field habit is locked in. |
| Payback period | 6 months | A frictionless mobile app dramatically reduces implementation timelines and CS training costs compared to our heavy desktop platform, accelerating time-to-value. |
| Investment required | ~$500,000 | Fully loaded cost (salary + overhead) of dedicating our 10-person cross-functional squad to this specific initiative for a complete 3-to-4 month quarter. |
| Expected return |$5M+ retained ARR; $1.5M net-new ARR | Secures at-risk Enterprise renewals (defensive return) while generating net-new mid-market acquisitions in Year 1 (offensive return), yielding a >10x ROI on the squad's initial build cost. |

> **The case in one paragraph:** We bet that launching a standalone mobile app completely isolated from the PM's web platform will establish a daily capture habit and drive mobile origination for field teams. This bet rests on the core assumption that UI bloat—not the act of reporting itself—is the primary barrier to adoption, and that a frictionless, 15-second utility will overcome the field's resistance to corporate software. The expected return is twofold: instantly securing multi-million-dollar enterprise renewals by proving we can bridge the field-to-office data gap, while simultaneously forging a lightweight, mobile-first wedge to win back the churning $5M–$50M mid-market. Our kill criterion is strict: if we do not see a sustained spike to 25% mobile origination for site photos and initial RFIs within 60 days of our beta launch, we kill the standalone app rather than sinking further engineering resources into complex backend translation layers.
